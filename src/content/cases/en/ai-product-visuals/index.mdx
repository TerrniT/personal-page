## Task

The catalogue grew faster than the team could prepare product cards. Managers assembled descriptions from attributes, wrote copy by hand and adapted it for different marketplaces. As a result, pages varied in style and some products waited weeks for publication.

## Solution

I built a generator that takes raw attributes and product photos and outputs a structured card: title, short and full description, benefit list, visual hints and copy variants for different channels.

Prompts and rules live in configuration so the team can change tone and structure without a developer. Drafts go to a review queue instead of being published automatically. Generation is connected to an n8n scenario: a new product in the spreadsheet starts card preparation.

## Results

The team gets a ready draft faster and spends time verifying facts instead of typing copy. The time-saving estimate is indicative: it depends on how complete the initial attributes are and on marketplace requirements.

## Limitations

AI does not know the real properties of a product unless they are in the input data. Fact, price and legal wording checks before publication are mandatory.
