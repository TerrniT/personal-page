## Task

The project team worked with a large volume of regulatory and project documentation. To find a requirement or check section completeness, engineers paged through hundreds of pages and relied on personal experience. New employees struggled to get up to speed.

## Solution

I built a RAG assistant over the internal document base: regulations, templates, project decisions and checklists. The system answers natural-language questions and always shows sources: specific documents and fragments.

Access is separated by roles and projects. Completeness checks use dedicated scenarios with formal rules: the assistant does not “invent” a document's presence but checks it against the base. Low-confidence answers are marked for review.

## Results

Engineers find requirements faster and spend less time on repetitive questions. The speed-up estimate is indicative and depends on how complete and up to date the knowledge base is.

## Limitations

Answer quality depends directly on document quality and freshness. The base needs an update process, and answers need spot checks by a specialist. Legally significant decisions remain with responsible engineers.
